About Me

Hi, I'm Fahad. I love running, and I love travelling. Running clears my mind and keeps me moving, and travel takes me to places I have never seen before.

But what I enjoy most is not just the places. It is the people. I love learning how others live, what they eat, how they spend their days, and what makes them happy. Every town and village has its own rhythm, and every person I meet has a story worth hearing.

This blog page is where I share my photos and my experiences from the places I visit. I hope my stories help you to see the world, and the people in it, a little differently.
